Standard Operating Procedure

01. Temperature Control Temper Wagyu patty at room temperature for exactly 12 minutes before searing. Do not exceed 15 minutes to prevent fat oxidation.

02. The Sear High heat cast iron. Sear for 2.5 minutes per side. Core temperature must reach exactly 52°C (Medium Rare) before resting.

03. Assembly Apply aioli to both bun halves. Place patty on heel, top with melted Gruyère, followed by onion jam. Crown and serve immediately.
